One model for molecules, crystals and proteins.

I'm Yuxuan Ren, a Ph.D. student at the National University of Singapore. I build generative models and molecular languages for science.

About

I work on AI for Science at the School of Computing, National University of Singapore, advised by Yatao Bian in the Blue Whale Lab.

My recent work asks how far one plain Transformer can go across atomic systems. SinAE is a single autoencoder that reconstructs molecules, crystals and proteins almost losslessly. STAMP is a molecular language that makes fragments easy to learn from and to edit.

I trained as a chemist at USTC before moving into machine learning, with time at Microsoft Research AI for Science and the Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence along the way.
